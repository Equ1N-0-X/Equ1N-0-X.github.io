OK SMOKE Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of OK SMOKE ("the app") handles your information.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your smoking logs (date, time, and count), goals, product names, prices and cigarettes per pack, purchase logs, and region and time zone settings are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to the developer's servers, and the developer cannot access or view it. No account is required to use the app.

2. Information collected by third parties for advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to show an ad at the bottom of the Home, This month, and Purchases screens. Google AdMob may collect the following information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and diagnose problems:

  • IP address (used to estimate your general location)
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier
  • Interactions such as ad views and taps
  • Crash and performance information

The app does not use the logs described in section 1 for advertising and does not share them with Google. The app does not request personalized (interest-based) ads. For details on how Google handles information, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required by law, the app asks for your choices about advertising. You can change them from "Ad privacy choices" in the app's Settings (when shown). You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.

4. Retention and deletion

The information in section 1 is erased when you delete logs in the app or delete the app from your device. The developer cannot restore erased information. Retention and deletion of the information in section 2 follow Google's policies.

5. Age

The app is intended for people who are of legal smoking age where they live. It is not intended for children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, email oksmoke.support@gmail.com.
