OK SMOKE Contact & Support

For questions, bug reports, or reports of inappropriate ads, email oksmoke.support@gmail.com. We respond in English, Japanese, and Korean.

When reporting a bug or an ad, please include your device model, OS version, the screen where it happened, and a screenshot if possible. You do not need to send your smoking logs or other personal information.

Where are my logs stored?

Only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers.

Can I move my logs to a new phone or keep them after reinstalling?

Not in the current version. Deleting the app also deletes your logs.

Can I change my ad choices?

The app shows a single ad at the bottom of the screen. You can change your ad choices from "Ad privacy choices" in Settings (when shown) or in your device's privacy settings.
